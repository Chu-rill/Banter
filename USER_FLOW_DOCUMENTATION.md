## Getting Started

### Create Your Account

1. **Sign Up**

   - Visit the Banter homepage
   - Click **"Get Started"**
   - Fill in your details:
     - Choose a username
     - Enter your email
     - Create a password (at least 8 characters)
   - Click **"Sign Up"**

2. **Verify Your Email**

   - Check your email inbox
   - Open the email from Banter
   - Click the verification link
   - You'll be automatically logged in!

3. **Or Sign in with Google**
   - Click **"Sign in with Google"**
   - Choose your Google account
   - You're in!

### First Time Login

1. **Go to the Login Page**

   - Click **"Sign In"** from the homepage
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click **"Sign In"**

2. **Forgot Your Password?**
   - Click **"Forgot Password?"**
   - Enter your email
   - Check your email for the reset link
   - Create a new password
   - Log in with your new password

## Chatting with Friends

### Adding Friends

1. **Search for Someone**

   - Click the **"Friends"** tab in the sidebar
   - Use the search bar at the top
   - Type their username or email
   - Click on their name when they appear

2. **Send a Friend Request**

   - Click **"Add Friend"**
   - They'll receive a notification
   - Wait for them to accept

3. **Accept Friend Requests**
   - Go to the **"Friends"** tab
   - Click **"Requests"**
   - See who wants to be your friend
   - Click **"Accept"** or **"Decline"**

### Messaging a Friend

1. **Start a Chat**

   - Click on a friend's name in the sidebar
   - Their chat opens on the right
   - Type your message in the box at the bottom
   - Press **Enter** to send

2. **You'll See:**

   - Your messages on the right (green)
   - Their messages on the left (gray)
   - A green dot if they're online
   - "Typing..." when they're writing

3. **Add Emojis**
   - Click the 😊 emoji button
   - Pick an emoji to add to your message

## Creating & Joining Rooms

### What's a Room?

A room is like a group chat where multiple people can talk together. Perfect for teams, projects, or friend groups!

### Create a New Room

1. **Click "Create Room"** in the sidebar

2. **Fill in the Details:**

   - **Room Name**: What do you want to call it?
   - **Description**: What's the room for? (optional)
   - **Type**:
     - **Public** - Anyone can join
     - **Private** - Only people you invite
   - **Mode**:
     - **Chat** - Text messages only
     - **Video** - For video calls
     - **Both** - Text and video

3. **Click "Create"**
   - Your room is ready!
   - You're automatically in the room
   - Start inviting people or chatting

### Join an Existing Room

1. **Find a Room**

   - Browse public rooms
   - Or get a room link from someone
   - Or search for a room name

2. **Join**
   - Click **"Join"** (for public rooms)
   - Or click **"Request to Join"** (for private rooms)
   - Wait for approval if it's private

### Chatting in Rooms

1. **Select the Room**

   - Click the room name in your Rooms list
   - The room chat opens

2. **Send Messages**

   - Type in the message box
   - Press **Enter** to send
   - Everyone in the room can see your message

3. **See Who's Typing**
   - You'll see "John and Sarah are typing..." at the bottom
   - Know when others are about to reply

### Managing Your Room

1. **Room Settings** (if you created the room)

   - Click the info icon (ⓘ) next to the room name
   - See three tabs:
     - **Details**: Room info
     - **Members**: Who's in the room
     - **Settings**: Change room options

2. **Add or Remove Members**

   - Go to the **Members** tab
   - Invite friends to join
   - Remove people if needed (room creators only)

3. **Leave a Room**

   - Open Room Settings
   - Click **"Leave Room"**
   - Confirm you want to leave

4. **Delete a Room** (creators only)
   - Open Room Settings → **Settings** tab
   - Scroll to **"Delete Room"**
   - This permanently deletes everything!
   - All messages and files will be gone

## Making Calls

### Starting a Voice or Video Call

1. **From a Friend Chat**

   - Open a chat with your friend
   - At the top, you'll see:
     - 📞 Phone icon = Voice call
     - 🎥 Video icon = Video call
   - Click one to start calling

2. **From a Room**

   - Open any room
   - Click the phone or video icon
   - Everyone in the room gets notified
   - Those who join will be in the call with you

3. **Allow Camera & Microphone**
   - Your browser will ask for permission
   - Click **"Allow"** so others can hear/see you

### Answering a Call

1. **You'll See a Popup**

   - Shows who's calling
   - Shows if it's voice or video
   - Two buttons: **Accept** or **Decline**

2. **Accept the Call**

   - Click **"Accept"**
   - Your browser may ask for mic/camera permission
   - Click **"Allow"**
   - You're now in the call!

3. **Decline the Call**
   - Click **"Decline"**
   - The popup disappears
   - The caller won't know you declined

### During a Call

**You'll See:**

- The other person's video (if video call)
- Your own video in a small box (bottom corner)
- Control buttons at the bottom

**Control Buttons:**

1. **🎥 Video** (toggle camera on/off)

   - Click to turn your camera off
   - Click again to turn it back on
   - Others see a black screen when it's off

2. **🎤 Microphone** (mute/unmute)

   - Click to mute yourself
   - Click again to unmute
   - Others can't hear you when muted

3. **📺 Share Screen**

   - Click to share your screen
   - Choose what to share (entire screen or just one window)
   - Click again to stop sharing

4. **☰ Participants** (see who's in the call)

   - See everyone on the call
   - See their video/audio status

5. **⊗ End Call** (hang up)
   - Click to leave the call
   - Others can keep talking if it's a group call

### Group Calls

When multiple people join:

- You'll see everyone's video in a grid
- Up to 100 people can join (depending on room settings)
- The person talking gets highlighted
- Everyone has their own controls

## Sharing Files

### Upload a File

1. **In Any Chat or Room**

   - Look for the 📎 paperclip icon
   - Click it to choose a file
   - Or drag & drop a file from your computer

2. **Select Your File**

   - Pick any file from your computer
   - Files can be up to 50MB
   - Supported: Images, videos, documents, PDFs, etc.

3. **File Uploads**
   - You'll see a progress bar
   - Once done, the file appears in the chat
   - Everyone can see and download it

### View or Download Files

**Images:**

- Show up as thumbnails in chat
- Click to see full size

**Videos:**

- Play directly in the chat
- Click play button

**Documents & Other Files:**

- Show as a file icon with the name
- Click to download

## Managing Your Account

### View Your Profile

1. **Click Your Profile** at the top of the sidebar
   - See your username, email, and profile picture
   - See when you joined

### Edit Your Profile

1. **Click "Edit Profile"**
2. **You Can Change:**
   - Your profile picture
   - Your username
   - Your bio (a short description about you)
3. **Click "Save"** when done

### Change Your Theme

- Click the 🌙 moon icon (or ☀️ sun icon) at the bottom of the sidebar
- Switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode
- Your choice is saved automatically

### Log Out

- Click **"Logout"** at the bottom of the sidebar
- You'll be taken back to the login page

## Quick Tips

### Communication Tips

✅ **DO:**

- Keep messages clear and friendly
- Use rooms for group topics
- Use direct messages for one-on-one chats
- Share files with descriptions
- Use video calls for complex discussions

❌ **DON'T:**

- Don't spam messages
- Don't share sensitive information in public rooms
- Don't forget to mute yourself when not speaking in calls

### Privacy Tips

🔒 **Stay Safe:**

- Use a strong password
- Verify your email address
- Only add people you know
- Use private rooms for sensitive topics
- Log out on shared computers
- Don't share your password with anyone

### Troubleshooting

**Can't Send Messages?**

- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page

**Call Not Working?**

- Make sure you allowed camera/microphone access
- Check your browser settings
- Try refreshing the page

**Forgot Password?**

- Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page
- Check your email for reset link

**Not Getting Emails?**

- Check your spam folder
- Make sure you entered the right email

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I use Banter on my phone?**
A: Yes! Banter works in any web browser on phones and tablets.

**Q: How many people can be in a room?**
A: Up to 100 people, depending on the room settings.

**Q: How many people can be on a video call?**
A: Multiple people can join, but call quality depends on everyone's internet connection.

**Q: Can I edit or delete my messages?**
A: This feature is coming soon!

**Q: Are my messages private?**
A: Direct messages are between you and your friend. Room messages can be seen by all room members.

**Q: Can I get notifications?**
A: Yes! You'll see notifications in the app. Browser notifications can be enabled in your browser settings.
